How long will a power station run a Wi-Fi router?

A very long time. A modem plus router uses about 15–25 W. After ~15% inverter loss, a 500 Wh station runs them roughly 20 hours, and a 1,000 Wh station roughly 40 hours.

  • Add a laptop (~50 W) and a phone charger: about 80 W total, so a 1,000 Wh station gives about 10 hours.

Tip: use a station's DC output with a 12 V router adapter to skip inverter losses. Remember your ISP's local equipment must also have power for service to work.
