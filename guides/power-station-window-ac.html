Can a portable power station run a window AC unit?

Usually yes for small units, but not for long. A 5,000–8,000 BTU window AC draws about 450–700 W running, with a start-up surge 2–3× higher. You need an inverter rated around 1,500 W+ to start it reliably.

Runtime at ~600 W (with ~50–70% compressor duty in Texas heat): about 2–3 hours on a 1 kWh station, 6–9 hours on a 3.6 kWh station. Inverter (variable-speed) ACs use less and start softer.

Best plan: cool one small room at night, and pair with solar or a generator for multi-day outages.

Estimates only; check your unit's manual and nameplate wattage.
