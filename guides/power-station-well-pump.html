Will a portable power station run a well pump?

Sometimes, but check the surge. A 1/2 HP well pump runs at about 800–1,000 W but needs 2,000–3,000 W to start. A 3/4 HP pump needs more.

  • You need an inverter with surge rating above the pump's starting watts, such as 3,000 W+ units.
  • Pumps run only a few minutes at a time, so a 2,000 Wh station can often cover a day of basic water for a household.
  • Many wells are 240 V, which most portable stations can't supply without a special adapter or a transfer switch; have an electrician confirm.

Estimates only; check your appliance nameplate and your station's continuous output rating.
