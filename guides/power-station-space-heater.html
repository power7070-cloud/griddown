Can a portable power station run a space heater?

Technically yes, but only for a short time. A typical space heater draws 1,500 W on high and about 750 W on low, so you need an inverter rated 1,800 W+ and lots of battery.

Runtime after ~15% inverter loss:

  • 1,000 Wh station: about 35 min on high or 70 min on low.
  • 2,000 Wh station: about 70 min on high or 2.3 hours on low.
  • 3,600 Wh station: about 2 hours on high or 4 hours on low.

Better plan: save the battery for an electric blanket or heated pad (50–100 W, so 8+ hours on 1,000 Wh), your furnace's blower and controls if it's gas, phones, lights, and the fridge. Heat one small closed room, and never run fuel-burning heaters indoors without ventilation and a CO alarm.

Estimates only; check your heater's label for its actual wattage.
