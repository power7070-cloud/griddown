Will an EcoFlow DELTA 2 run a refrigerator?

Yes. The DELTA 2 has a 1024 Wh battery and an 1800 W AC inverter, which easily covers a typical fridge's ~150 W running draw and its brief start-up surge.

A full-size fridge uses roughly 1.2–1.8 kWh per day. After inverter losses (~85% usable), expect about 12–17 hours of fridge-only runtime. Add a 400 W of solar panel and, in Houston's ~5 peak sun hours, you can roughly break even on fridge use on a sunny day.

Tips: keep the door shut, set the fridge to 38°F before the storm, and plug in only the fridge overnight.
