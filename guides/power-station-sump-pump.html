Can a power station run a sump pump?

Yes, for a typical 1/3 HP pump, if the station has a big enough inverter. A 1/3 HP pump runs about 400–800 W but surges to 1,300–2,000 W for a second at startup, so pick a station with a surge rating of 2,000 W or more.

Pumps only run when water rises, often 10–20% of the time in a storm:

  • 1,000 Wh station: about 2–3 hours of pump run time, roughly 12–20 hours of storm coverage.
  • 2,000 Wh station: about 4–6 hours of pump run time, or a day or more of coverage.

Tips: hard-wired pumps need a transfer switch, so don't backfeed an outlet. Plug-in pumps can connect directly. A separate battery-backup pump is often cheaper for long outages.

Estimates only. Check your pump's nameplate for running and surge watts.
