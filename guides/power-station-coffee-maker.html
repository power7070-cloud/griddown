Will a portable power station run a coffee maker?

Yes, if the station has an inverter of 1,000 W or more. A drip coffee maker pulls about 900–1,200 W while heating, but only for ~5–10 minutes per pot.

  • One 10-minute pot uses roughly 150–200 Wh, so a 1,000 Wh station brews about 4–5 pots.
  • Single-serve pods (Keurig) draw ~1,400 W briefly, about 30–50 Wh per cup.
  • Small 500 W stations usually can't handle drip makers; try a 12 V/USB kettle or a pour-over with a camp stove.

Tip: skip the warming plate, which keeps drawing ~75 W.
