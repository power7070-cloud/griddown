Will a portable power station run a microwave?

Yes, if the inverter is big enough. A "700 W" microwave typically draws 1,000–1,300 W from the outlet, so you need a station with a continuous AC rating of at least 1,200–1,500 W.

  • 1,000 Wh station: roughly 40 minutes of total microwave run time after ~15% inverter loss.
  • 2,000 Wh station: roughly 80 minutes.

Reheating a meal takes 2–3 minutes, so even a modest battery covers many meals. Microwaves have a short startup surge, so choose a station with a surge rating above 2,000 W if possible.

Estimates only; check the wattage label on your microwave.
