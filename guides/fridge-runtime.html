How long will a power station run a refrigerator?

A full-size fridge averages ~1.2–1.8 kWh/day (≈150W running, ~1/3 duty cycle). A 1 kWh station runs it ~12–18 hours; a 3.6 kWh EcoFlow DELTA Pro ~2–2.5 days; a 13.5 kWh Powerwall ~7+ days fridge-only. Use the calculator for your exact mix.
