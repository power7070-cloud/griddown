Will a portable power station run a CPAP all night?

Yes, almost any 500 Wh+ station can. Most CPAP machines draw about 30–60 W with the humidifier off, and roughly 70–110 W with the heated humidifier and heated tube on.

For an 8-hour night, after ~15% inverter loss:

  • Humidifier off (~40 W): about 380 Wh per night, so a 1,000 Wh station gives roughly 2 nights.
  • Humidifier on (~90 W): about 850 Wh per night, so a 1,000 Wh station gives roughly 1 night.

Tips: turn off the humidifier during outages, and use your CPAP maker's DC cable or power adapter from the station's 12 V / DC output to skip inverter losses (often 20–30% more runtime). Add a solar panel to recharge during the day.

Estimates only; check your machine's power supply rating and talk to your doctor or equipment supplier about outage plans.
