Will a power station run a chest freezer?

Yes, easily. A modern chest freezer averages about 40–80 W over a day (the compressor cycles), which is about 1–2 kWh per day.

  • 1,000 Wh station: roughly 12–18 hours.
  • 2,000 Wh station: roughly 1–1.5 days.
  • With a 200 W solar panel recharging, a 1,000 Wh station can often run a freezer indefinitely in sunny weather.

Tips: a full, closed chest freezer holds frozen food about 48 hours without power, so run it in short cycles (about 1 hour on, 3 off) to stretch a small battery. Check the startup surge, often 300–600 W.
